# A safe mock interview

Use these synthetic questions with a partner before a real call. Do not import this page as your actual biography. No result or achievement below belongs to a real candidate.

1. “Please tell me about a project you found challenging and explain your own role.”
2. “I would like to hear how you handled a disagreement in the team.”
3. “Walk me through the trade-offs in your design, and how you checked the outcome.”
4. “Looking back, I am interested in what you would improve next time.”
5. “Could you connect that experience to the requirements of this role?”

Try question 3 with a natural pause after “design”; the earlier clause should remain part of the question. Try questions 2 and 4 without rising intonation; they are still requests. Check that English and Chinese remain paired, and that any answer distinguishes your supplied experiences from proposals.

For document grounding, create a short project summary using your own verified information: goal, your exact role, actions, measured results, limitations, and source pages. Import it with a CV and role description. Ask the same questions again and inspect which excerpts the software uses. Remove details you do not want sent before choosing a cloud answer model.

For history, leave auto-save off, click Save conversation, open History, and export a TXT or Markdown copy. Close and reopen the application, then check the saved record without starting listening. If enabling auto-save, understand that it writes plaintext local conversation files.
